About

My humanities background has instilled in me with a deep curiosity about complex problems. Throughout my career, I have been driven by a desire to understand diverse situations and find meaningful solutions, whether analyzing country-specific contexts for humanitarian aid projects across Central Asia or working directly with families in crisis as a mediator.

These experiences taught me the value of critical thinking, careful listening, and translating complicated situations into actionable paths forward. Now, I'm channeling that same analytical mindset into a Master's in Data Science, turning raw information into compelling narratives grounded in evidence. I'm excited to bring human-centered problem-solving into the world of data and work at the intersection of analysis, storytelling, and impact.
